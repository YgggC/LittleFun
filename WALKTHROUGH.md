## 1. Game Overview & Core Mechanics

### The Premise
You awaken at **07:00** in a quaint Bavarian village with only **€5** in your pocket and **80/100 Stamina**. Your day ends at **24:00 (Midnight)**. To survive a standard loop, you must attend two university courses and keep your stamina above 0. However, strange inconsistencies linger across town—Professor Horton has died under mysterious circumstances, students suffer from memory lapses, and the day resets back to 07:00 when midnight strikes.

### Core Resources
| Resource | HUD Indicator | Initial Value | Rules & Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time** | `TIME` (Clock) | `07:00` | Advances with actions (usually +1h or +2h). Midnight (24:00) ends the day. Fast travel takes **0 time**. |
| **Money** | `MONEY` (€) | `€5` | Used for food (€2 Brezel, €4 Schnitzel), cat food (€1), and the wishing fountain (€1). Earned via jobs or investigation. |
| **Stamina** | `STAMINA` (♥) | `80/100` | Reaching 0 causes immediate physical collapse and ends your day. Restored by food, sleeping, praying, or meditating. |
| **Courses** | `COURSES` (🎓) | `0/2` | Course 1 (08:00–10:00) and Course 2 (13:00–15:00). Both must be finished before midnight to win the day. |
| **Karma** | Bag Item (🕊️) | `0` | Good Guy Cards stored in your **Backpack**. Earned through kindness (feeding Momo, reporting the crime). Spent at Church for miracles. |
| **Cycle** | Medallion | `1` | Increments each time the day resets. Certain events (records, dialogue, the Broken Clock) unlock on higher cycles. |

### 1. School (Academy Hall & Lecture Rooms)
*The central hub of academics, faculty drama, and physical crime evidence.*
* **Attend Course 1 (Logic & Models)**:
  * Alone: Requires 2 hours, consumes **15 stamina**. Available anytime, but intended for 08:00–10:00.
  * With Jason: Talk to Jason -> Attend together: Consumes only **10 stamina**, 2 hours.
* **Attend Course 2 (Responsible AI)**:
  * Alone: Requires 2 hours, consumes **15 stamina**. Available anytime, but intended for 13:00–15:00.
  * With Jason: Talk to Jason -> Attend together: Consumes only **10 stamina**, 2 hours.
* **Inspect Horton's Podium**: Takes 1 hour, consumes **10 stamina**. Yields **Bloodstained Chalk** (physical clue).
* **Search Horton's Office**: Takes 1 hour, consumes **5 stamina**. Discovers **PROJECT ECHO experiment records** (Record 1 in Cycle 1, up to Record 4 in Cycle 4+).
* **Talk to Jason**:
  * *Ask about Horton's argument*: 1 hour. Provides Jason's eyewitness statement (heard Horton and Rebecca quarreling).
* **Talk to Rebecca**:
  * *Accept Rebecca's little snack*: Free, restores **+15 stamina** (Only available when stamina is below 25; once per day).
  * *Ask about Horton's research*: 1 hour. Unlocks Rebecca's account of PROJECT ECHO.
  * *Confront Rebecca*: Appears after you have Jason's statement or verified lab evidence. Allows choosing between exposing her or accepting **€6 hush money**.

### 2. Supermarket
*Opening Hours: **08:00 – 20:00***
* **Buy Brezel**: Costs **€2**. Adds 1 Brezel to your bag (+20 stamina when eaten; eating consumes 0 time).
* **Buy Canned Cat Food**: Costs **€1**. Adds 1 Cat food to your bag (used to feed Momo).
* **Restock Shelves (Shift Work)**: Takes 2 hours, consumes **20 stamina**. Pays **€3**.

### 3. Restaurant (Bavarian Gasthaus)
*Opening Hours: **11:00 – 22:00***
* **Order Schnitzel Feast**: Costs **€4**, takes 1 hour. Restores **+40 stamina**.
* **Wash Dishes (Shift Work)**: Takes 2 hours, consumes **25 stamina**. Pays **€4**.

### 4. Stray Cat Alley (Momo)
*Always accessible. The elusive black cat Momo watches silently over the loop.*
* **Feed Momo**: Consumes 1 canned cat food (0 time). Yields progressive rewards:
  * **Feed 1**: Momo brings a dead mouse and **+1 Good Guy Card**.
  * **Feed 2**: Momo places **Rebecca's bloodstained ring** at your feet.
  * **Every 3rd feed (3, 6, 9)**: Momo brings another dead mouse.
  * **Every 4th feed (4, 8)**: Momo purrs approvingly; grants **+1 Good Guy Card**.
* **Eat Raw Dead Mouse**: Consumes 1 dead mouse (0 time). Restores **+15 stamina** (Unlocks `Iron Stomach`).
* **Receive The Broken Clock** *(Cycle 5+ only)*: Momo gifts the ancient timepiece inscribed with `ECHO — SUBJECT 005`.

### 5. City Park
*Always accessible. A peaceful refuge with shady benches and mischievous crows.*
* **Sleep on the Bench**: Takes 2 hours.
  * First 2 sleeps: Restores **+25 stamina**.
  * Subsequent sleeps: Restores **+15 stamina**.
  * *Crow Hazard*: 25% chance a crow steals **€1**. If it steals your very last euro, you earn the `Rock Bottom` achievement.
* **Plant Mouse Fertilizer**: Requires 1 dead mouse (0 time). Sprouts a magical golden flower.
* **Harvest Golden Flower**: Once per day. Yields **€4**. (If Momo was fed on the same day, unlocks `Momo's Strategist`).

### 6. Saint Mary's Church
*Always accessible. Cold stone walls and echoed chimes.*
* **Sit in Quiet Vigil**: Takes 1 hour, consumes 0 stamina. Safe way to pass time.
* **Pray for a Miracle**: Requires **1 Good Guy Card (Karma)**. Takes 1 hour. Restores **+25 stamina** and grants **€2**. (Unlocks `Saint of the Streets`).
* **Observe Altar**: Flavor inspection; listen to the bells.

### 7. Alpine River
*Always accessible. Melts down from the Bavarian peaks.*
* **Search Riverbank**: Takes 1 hour, consumes **5 stamina**. Discovers **Horton's Necktie** tangled in the reeds.
* **Meditate**: Takes 1 hour.
  * First 2 sessions: Restores **+8 stamina**.
  * Subsequent sessions: Restores **+5 stamina**. (Meditating twice in a day unlocks `Alpine Philosopher`).

### 8. Police Station
*Always accessible. Officer Erik is on duty analyzing forensic evidence.*
* **Test Bloodstained Ring**: Requires Rebecca's ring from Momo. Takes 1 hour. Erik confirms **Horton's blood** on the setting.
* **Test Horton's Necktie**: Requires the tie from the river. Takes 1 hour. Erik confirms **Rebecca's fingerprints** on the silk.
* **Open Detective Journal**: Review clues, evidence board, and deduction cards.
* **File Formal Police Report**: Unlocks once all 3 deductions are correctly solved. Takes 1 hour. Erik awards **€8 reward** and **+2 Good Guy Cards**. Closes case as `Reported`.

### 9. Wishing Fountain
*Always accessible. Glistening spring water in the village square.*
* **Toss a Coin**: Costs **€1** (Maximum 3 bets per day).
  * Random outcome: Returns **€0**, **€1**, or **€2**.
  * Winning €2 twice in a row unlocks the `Fountain Gambler` achievement.
* **Watch the Water**: Flavor inspection (0 time).

## 3. The Mystery: How to Solve & Close the Case

### Step 1: Gather the Evidence
To unlock the deduction board in your **Detective Journal**, you must obtain:
1. **Jason's Eyewitness Testimony**: Go to School -> Talk to Jason -> *Ask about Horton's argument* (He heard furious shouting between Rebecca and Horton before morning classes).
2. **Rebecca's Admission of Motive**: Go to School -> Talk to Rebecca -> *Ask about Horton's research* (She reveals Horton was conducting unapproved time-loop experiments on students).
3. **At Least One Verified Forensic Clue**:
   * *Method A (Necktie)*: Search Alpine River -> Bring tie to Police Station -> Test Horton's Necktie (Confirms Rebecca's fingerprints).
   * *Method B (Ring)*: Buy cat food at Supermarket -> Feed Momo twice at Stray Cat Alley -> Take ring to Police Station -> Test Bloodstained Ring (Confirms Horton's blood).
   * *(Optional Clue)*: Inspect Horton's podium at School -> Obtain Bloodstained Chalk.

### Step 2: Solve the 3 Deductions
Open your **Detective Journal** (`📓 Journal` in the top bar). Answer the three questions:

* **Deduction 1: Why did Rebecca confront Horton?**
  * [ ] Option 1: He charged too much for school lunches.
  * [x] **Option 2: He was experimenting on students with PROJECT ECHO without consent.** *(CORRECT)*
  * [ ] Option 3: Jason asked her to steal grades.
* **Deduction 2: What does the physical evidence establish?**
  * [x] **Option 1: Rebecca had contact with Horton during a struggle.** *(CORRECT)*
  * [ ] Option 2: The death was premeditated.
  * [ ] Option 3: Jason committed the crime.
* **Deduction 3: Which explanation best fits the evidence?**
  * [ ] Option 1: Rebecca planned a killing for money.
  * [x] **Option 2: A confrontation over the experiments escalated and Horton died accidentally.** *(CORRECT)*
  * [ ] Option 3: Horton left town voluntarily.

*(Note: An incorrect deduction costs 1 hour of game time. Correct answers unlock `Curious Detective`.)*

### Step 3: Choose Your Resolution Path
Once all three deductions are solved, you can conclude the case in one of two ways:

#### Path A: The Path of Justice (Report to Police)
1. Go to the **Police Station**.
2. Click **File formal report** (Takes 1 hour).
3. Officer Erik accepts the case as an accidental death caused during an altercation.
4. **Rewards**: **€8 Cash**, **+2 Good Guy Cards**, Case Status: `Reported`.
5. **Achievement**: Unlocks `Sherlock of Bavaria`.

#### Path B: The Path of Complicity (Accept Hush Money)
1. Go to the **School** and click **Talk to Rebecca**.
2. Select **Speak privately about Horton's death** (`Ask what happened during their confrontation`).
3. Rebecca breaks down in tears, confessing she pushed him into the podium in self-defense and panicked.
4. Click **Accept €6 hush money** (Takes 1 hour).
5. **Consequences**: **+€6 Cash**, **−1 Good Guy Card**, Case Status: `Hush`.
6. **Achievement**: Unlocks `Partner in Crime`.

## 4. Breaking the Loop: The True Ending

To permanently break the time loop and reach the **True Ending: "Tomorrow Is Yours"**, follow these specific steps:

```
[Reach Cycle 5] ──> [Stray Cat Alley] ──> [Receive Broken Clock from Momo]
                            │
                            ▼
               [Backpack / Examine Clock]
                            │
                            ▼
              [Click "Break the Loop"]
                            │
                            ▼
           [Complete Course 1 & Course 2]
                            │
                            ▼
              [Survive Until 24:00 (Midnight)]
                            │
                            ▼
               ★ TRUE ENDING UNLOCKED ★
```

1. **Advance to Cycle 5**: If you are on an earlier cycle, reset or finish days until your medallion displays **CYCLE 5**.
2. **Collect the Watch**: Visit **Stray Cat Alley**. Momo will be waiting with a golden glow. Click **Accept Momo’s mysterious gift** to obtain **The Broken Clock** (Unlocks `The Cat Knows` and `Subject No. 005`).
3. **Examine & Shatter the Anchor**:
   * Open your **Backpack (`🎒 Bag`)**.
   * Under Evidence, click **Examine the clock**.
   * Click the red button: **Break the Loop / Shatter the clock mechanism**.
   * The glass and gears shatter, severing Subject 005’s connection to PROJECT ECHO (Unlocks `Break the Loop`).
4. **Fulfill Graduation Duties**: You cannot leave until your day's work is done. You must attend and complete **Course 1** and **Course 2**.
5. **Reach Midnight**: Maintain stamina above 0 and pass time until **24:00**.
6. **The Result**: The midnight bells ring true. For the first time, midnight does not rewind to 07:00. The True Ending modal appears, granting the prestigious **Tomorrow Is Yours** achievement!

## 6. Optimal Step-by-Step Playthrough Routes

To earn all 34 achievements efficiently without unnecessary grinding, follow this 5-Cycle master roadmap:

```
[CYCLE 1] ──> Model Student + Full Police Investigation (Sherlock)
    │
[CYCLE 2] ──> Penny Pincher + Barely Survived (Zero Spending Challenge)
    │
[CYCLE 3] ──> Cat Master & Big Spender (Nine Lives + Momo's Strategist)
    │
[CYCLE 4] ──> The Rogue & Misfortune (Partner in Crime + Rock Bottom)
    │
[CYCLE 5] ──> True Ending (Shatter Clock + Tomorrow Is Yours)
```

### Route 1: Cycle 1 — The Ideal Academic & Detective
*Focus: On-time classes with Jason, solving the murder, unlocking Sherlock of Bavaria, and finishing with high stamina.*
1. **07:00**: Go to School -> Talk to Jason -> Eyewitness Statement (+1h -> 08:00).
2. **08:00**: Attend Course 1 together with Jason (+2h -> 10:00). *(Unlocks `Study Buddies`, `First Day at School`).*
3. **10:00**: Inspect Podium (+1h, -10 stamina -> 11:00, Bloodstained Chalk found).
4. **11:00**: Go to Alpine River -> Search riverbank (+1h, -5 stamina -> 12:00, Horton's Tie found).
5. **12:00**: Go to Supermarket -> Buy 1 Brezel (€2) and 1 Cat food (€1) -> €2 remaining.
6. **12:00**: Go to Police Station -> Test Horton's Necktie (+1h -> 13:00).
7. **13:00**: Return to School -> Attend Course 2 together with Jason (+2h -> 15:00). *(Unlocks `Model Student`, `Best Study Partners`).*
8. **15:00**: Talk to Rebecca -> Ask about research (+1h -> 16:00).
9. **16:00**: Search Horton's Office (+1h, -5 stamina -> 17:00, Record A found).
10. **17:00**: Open Journal -> Solve Deductions 1, 2, and 3. *(Unlocks `Curious Detective`).*
11. **17:00**: Go to Police Station -> File formal report (+1h, +€8 reward, +2 Karma -> 18:00). *(Unlocks `Sherlock of Bavaria`).*
12. **18:00**: Go to Stray Cat Alley -> Feed Momo (+1 mouse, +1 Karma). Eat Brezel (+20 stamina). Eat dead mouse (+15 stamina). *(Unlocks `Iron Stomach`).*
13. **18:00**: Go to Church -> Pray for a miracle (+1h, +25 stamina, +€2 -> 19:00). *(Unlocks `Saint of the Streets`).*
14. **19:00**: Go to Restaurant -> Order Schnitzel feast (+1h, +40 stamina -> 20:00).
15. **20:00–24:00**: Pass time at Church (Quiet Vigil). Midnight strikes with 100 stamina and 3 Good Guy Cards. *(Unlocks `Perfect Survivor`, `Good Neighbor`).*

### Route 2: Cycle 2 — The Frugal & Exhausted Survivor
*Focus: `Penny Pincher` (€0 spent), `Barely Survived` (stamina ≤ 10), `King of Tardiness`, and `Alpine Philosopher`.*
1. **07:00**: Start Cycle 2. *(Unlocks `Déjà Vu`).*
2. **07:00**: Do NOT spend a single euro all day!
3. **07:00–10:00**: Meditate at Alpine River twice (+2h -> 09:00). *(Unlocks `Alpine Philosopher`).* Walk around / vigil until 10:00.
4. **10:00**: Go to School -> Attend Course 1 alone (10:00–12:00, -15 stamina). (Attended late!).
5. **12:00**: Sit in Church vigil (+1h -> 13:00).
6. **13:00**: Search Riverbank (-5 stamina, +1h -> 14:00).
7. **14:00**: Pass time until 15:00 in Church.
8. **15:00**: Attend Course 2 alone (15:00–17:00, -15 stamina). (Attended late!). *(Unlocks `King of Tardiness`).*
9. **17:00–22:00**: Search podium (-10 stamina) and office (-5 stamina, Record B found).
10. **Tuning Stamina**: Check your stamina meter. Sleep or vigil to calibrate. Use River searching (-5 stamina) until stamina is **between 5 and 10**.
11. **24:00**: Let the clock hit midnight with €5 unspent, both courses done, and ≤10 stamina. *(Unlocks `Penny Pincher`, `Barely Survived`).*

---

### Route 3: Cycle 3 — The Feline Patron & Farm Tycoon
*Focus: `Cat Lover` (feed 5 times), `Nine Lives` (feed 9 times), and `Momo's Strategist`.*
1. **07:00**: Start Cycle 3. *(Unlocks `Loop Explorer`).*
2. **07:00**: Search Horton's Office (+1h, Record C found). *(Unlocks `Echo Researcher`).*
3. **08:00**: Work at Supermarket (+2h, -20 stamina, earns €3 -> €8 total).
4. **11:00**: Work at Restaurant (+2h, -25 stamina, earns €4 -> €12 total). *(Unlocks `Workaholic`).*
5. **13:00**: Complete both courses (alone or with Jason) before evening.
6. **17:00**: Supermarket -> Buy 9 cans of cat food (€9).
7. **18:00**: Go to Stray Cat Alley:
   * Feed 1: Momo brings mouse + card.
   * Feed 2: Momo brings Rebecca's ring. *(Unlocks `Silent Witness`, `Mastermind's Tribute`).*
   * Feed 3, 4, 5: Feed Momo. *(Unlocks `Cat Lover`).*
   * Feed 6, 7, 8, 9: Feed Momo. *(Unlocks `Nine Lives`).*
8. **19:00**: Go to City Park -> Plant dead mouse fertilizer -> Harvest golden flower (+€4). *(Unlocks `Momo's Strategist`).*
9. **20:00–24:00**: Relax until midnight.

---

### Route 4: Cycle 4 — The Corrupt & Unlucky
*Focus: `Partner in Crime`, `Rebecca's Kindness`, `Big Eater`, and `Rock Bottom`.*
1. **07:00**: Intentionally lower stamina (work at supermarket, river search) until stamina drops below 25.
2. **10:00**: Go to School -> Talk to Rebecca -> Click *Accept Rebecca's little snack*. *(Unlocks `Rebecca's Kindness`).*
3. **11:00**: Eat Brezel, Schnitzel, and mice across the day. *(Unlocks `Big Eater`).*
4. **13:00**: Attend both courses.
5. **16:00**: Search office (Record D found). Talk to Jason (eyewitness) + inspect podium (chalk).
6. **17:00**: Talk to Rebecca -> Confront her with the evidence -> Select **Accept €6 hush money**. *(Unlocks `Partner in Crime`).*
7. **18:00**: Toss coins at fountain or buy items until you have **exactly €1**.
8. **19:00**: Go to City Park -> Sleep on the bench repeatedly (3 times unlocks `God of Sleep`). Keep sleeping until the crow steals your final €1. *(Unlocks `Rock Bottom`).*
9. **24:00**: Day ends.

---

### Route 5: Cycle 5 — The True Ending: Breaking the Echo
*Focus: `The Fifth Morning`, `The Cat Knows`, `Subject No. 005`, `Break the Loop`, and `Tomorrow Is Yours`.*
1. **07:00**: Awaken on Cycle 5. *(Unlocks `The Fifth Morning`).*
2. **07:00**: Go immediately to **Stray Cat Alley**.
3. **07:00**: Momo is waiting with a gleaming object under its paws. Click **Accept Momo’s mysterious gift**. You receive **The Broken Clock** with the inscription `ECHO — SUBJECT 005`. *(Unlocks `The Cat Knows`, `Subject No. 005`).*
4. **07:00**: Open your **Backpack (`🎒 Bag`)**, click **Examine the clock**, and select **Break the Loop / Shatter the clock mechanism**. The mechanism shatters, ending PROJECT ECHO’s temporal anchor. *(Unlocks `Break the Loop`).*
5. **08:00**: Attend **Course 1** with Jason (08:00–10:00).
6. **13:00**: Attend **Course 2** with Jason (13:00–15:00).
7. **Evening**: Relax, eat well, and pass time peacefully.
8. **24:00**: Midnight strikes. Because the anchor was shattered, time moves forward into a genuine tomorrow. The story concludes with the True Ending! *(Unlocks `Tomorrow Is Yours`).*
